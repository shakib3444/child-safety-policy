Child Safety Standards & Abuse Prevention Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Developer: SeedLift

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

SeedLift is committed to maintaining a safe digital environment for all users. We strictly prohibit any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or any behavior that endangers minors.

2. Strict Prohibition of CSAE Content

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation content
  • Sexualized content involving minors
  • Grooming or attempting to exploit minors
  • Sharing or distributing CSAM

Violation results in:

  • Immediate account suspension or ban
  • Content removal
  • Reporting to authorities when required
